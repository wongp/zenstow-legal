Workflow

BinFlow follows the day instead of fighting it.

1. Set up the workspace

Create your business and locations

Start with a business workspace, then add the places inventory actually moves through so teams share the same map.

2. Work the floor

Scan items and record adjustments

Keep stock actions close to the task at hand, whether you are receiving, correcting counts, or moving inventory between bins.

3. Review what changed

Use the activity feed to stay aligned

When something looks off, the team can review recent inventory events instead of reconstructing the day from memory.
