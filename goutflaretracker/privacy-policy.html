Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Gout Flare Tracker is operated by Patrick Wong. Contact: zenstow.business@gmail.com.

Your health data stays on your device. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to profile you.

1. Data We Collect

The information you enter - flare logs, symptom notes, foods, pain levels, body parts, and preferences - is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to our servers.

If you enable notifications, your notification preferences and scheduling history are also stored locally on your device.

We also collect limited app-usage analytics through a third-party analytics provider, such as screen views, taps, feature usage, timestamps, and basic device or browser information. Depending on your device and network settings, the provider may also receive technical information such as an IP address. We do not send your flare logs, symptom notes, food entries, or other health content to analytics providers.

2. How We Use It

Your data is used only to provide app functionality: saving logs, showing history, generating pattern insights, scheduling optional reminders, and understanding how the app is used so we can improve reliability and the product experience. It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any third-party commercial purpose. It is never used to determine employment or insurance eligibility, and is never shared without your explicit action.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app may request notification permission and schedule local notifications on your device during active flares or recovery periods. This permission is optional - the app works without it. You can turn it off at any time in your device settings. Notification content is generated on your device and not sent to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party analytics providers to process the limited usage data described above. They help us understand which features are used and where the app can be improved. The providers may change over time, but we only use services that support this limited analytics purpose.

The app includes Firebase Core for configuration purposes. Firebase Core does not collect personal data in our implementation. See Firebase's privacy information for details.

In-app subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple and Google handle purchases under their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. To request deletion of any information you sent us directly (e.g., by email), contact zenstow.business@gmail.com.

We keep limited analytics data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, then delete or aggregate it when practical.

6. Children

Gout Flare Tracker is not directed to children under 13.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data. For California residents: we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when we post the updated policy with a new effective date.
