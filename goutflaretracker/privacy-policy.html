Short version: Gout Flare Tracker is designed as a local-first health log. The flare, symptom, food, body-part, and note information you enter is stored on your device in the current version of the app. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Gout Flare Tracker handles information when you use the mobile or web versions of the app and related support pages.

2. Information You May Enter

Gout Flare Tracker lets you record information that may be considered health-related or sensitive, including:

  • whether you had a gout flare or symptom-free day
  • pain level
  • affected body parts
  • foods or drinks you choose to log
  • notes about symptoms, timing, or context
  • historical flare entries and confidence labels
  • app preferences, onboarding status, and recent option lists

3. Local Storage

In the current version, your logs and preferences are stored locally on your device using app storage such as SQLite and local preferences. The app uses local identifiers, such as a log ID and a local device label, to save and update entries on your device.

Deleting the app or clearing app data through your device settings may remove locally stored information. If your platform offers backup or restore features, your device or operating system provider may handle backups according to its own settings and policies.

4. Information Collected Automatically

Gout Flare Tracker includes Firebase Core initialization so the app can use Firebase-backed configuration if needed. The current app code does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, advertising SDKs, or in-app purchase packages.

App stores and platform providers, such as Apple and Google, may collect download, crash, purchase, device, and diagnostic information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. How Information Is Used

Information you enter is used to provide app functionality, including:

  • saving daily and historical flare logs
  • showing history and symptom-free day patterns
  • generating cautious food-pattern insights from your own entries
  • remembering custom body-part and food options
  • supporting troubleshooting when you contact us

6. Sharing and Sale of Data

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising.

In the current version, health log data you enter is not transmitted by Gout Flare Tracker to our servers. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or service integrity.

7. Permissions

The current version of Gout Flare Tracker does not request location, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, photo library, or Health Connect permissions for core functionality.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, reset app data where available, or uninstall the app.

To request deletion of information you sent to us by email or support request, contact zenstow.business@gmail.com. Please include enough detail for us to identify the request.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate for a local-first app. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device.

10. Health and Medical Information

Gout Flare Tracker is a personal tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

11. Children

Gout Flare Tracker is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about certain data practices. To make a request, contact us using the email below.
